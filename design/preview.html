[ 02 ] · DISPLAY FONT

Eight candidates — pick one for headlines

Each card shows the same headline + section subtitle in a different display face. All eight are free, open-source, and on Google Fonts — no licensing cost. Pair with Geist Mono for captions/metadata across the board.

01 · Bricolage Grotesque Atipo Foundry · 2023 free · OFL variable · grade + width
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Geometric humanist sans with variable grade. Feels editorial, slightly mechanical. Reads premium without trying. Strong recommendation.
02 · Unbounded Yermolayev · 2023 free · OFL display · geometric
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Tight, futuristic, mechanical. Strong vertical stress. This is the most "2040" of the set — closest to what aerospace + robotics studios use today.
03 · Syne Bonjour Monde · 2018 free · OFL display · editorial
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Geometric with character. Subtle quirks (the M, the A) give it personality. Used by Linear-adjacent design studios. Modern but not trendy.
04 · Space Grotesk Florian Karsten · 2018 free · OFL display · technical
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
The de-facto choice for crypto / engineering / dev-tool sites. Safe. Strong. Slightly overused, which is the only argument against it.
05 · Geist Vercel · 2023 free · OFL variable · neutral
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Vercel's house typeface. Built for screens, exceptional rendering on Mac/iPhone. Pair with Geist Mono. Neutral — relies on layout to feel distinct.
06 · Anton Vernon Adams · 2011 free · OFL display · condensed
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Tall, condensed, brutalist. Used by skate / streetwear / fashion. Massive presence on the hero. Maybe too loud for a robotics team — depends on your taste.
07 · Archivo Black Omnibus-Type · 2012 free · OFL display · heavy
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
Heavy industrial sans. Reads "engineering manual cover." Strong personality. Pairs well with mono captions. Less editorial than Bricolage.
08 · DM Sans Colophon / Google · 2014 free · OFL variable · neutral
MAKEMINDS
ROBOTICS
FTC TEAM 23786 — EDISON, NJ
The safest, most neutral option. Used by Notion, Vercel docs, Linear. If you want the design to disappear so the motion + layout carry the personality, this is the pick.
